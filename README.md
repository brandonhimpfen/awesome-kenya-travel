Kenya is a premier East African safari destination featuring iconic wildlife reserves, the Great Rift Valley, Mount Kenya alpine heights, and pristine Indian Ocean coastlines. This list is intended for wildlife safari travelers, nature conservationists, mountain hikers, and coastal explorers.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, eTA requirements, and wildlife authorities.

- [Magical Kenya (Kenya Tourism Board)](https://www.magicalkenya.com/) - Official national tourism portal featuring destination highlights, travel itineraries, and cultural guides.
- [Kenya eTA Portal](https://etakenya.go.ke/) - Official electronic travel authorization system required for all international visitors entering Kenya.
- [Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS)](https://kws.go.ke/) - State corporation managing public national parks, marine reserves, conservation updates, and park fee structures.
- [Kenya Meteorological Department](https://www.meteo.go.ke/) - Official national weather agency providing regional climate tracking, seasonal rain forecasts, and weather warnings.

## Transportation

Navigating domestic aviation, standard gauge railways, safari vehicles, and coastal transit.

- [Kenya Airways](https://www.kenya-airways.com/) - National flag carrier operating domestic flights between Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, and regional safari airstrips.
- [SGR Madaraka Express (Kenya Railways)](https://krc.co.ke/) - Modern passenger railway line connecting Nairobi with the coastal port city of Mombasa.
- [Safarilink Aviation](https://www.flysafarilink.com/) - Premier domestic airline specializing in scheduled flights directly to national parks and reserves including Maasai Mara and Amboseli.

## Destinations & Regions

### Nairobi & Central Circuit

- [Nairobi National Park Guide (KWS)](https://kws.go.ke/) - Official visitor guide for Kenya's oldest game park featuring urban-edge wildlife viewing, black rhinos, and safari walks.
- [David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust](https://www.sheldrickwildlifetrust.org/) - Orphaned elephant rescue and rehabilitation center located near Nairobi National Park.

### Maasai Mara & Rift Valley

- [Maasai Mara National Reserve Portal](https://kws.go.ke/) - Visitor portal for the legendary savannah ecosystem, big cat tracking, and the Great Wildebeest Migration.
- [Lake Nakuru National Park Guide](https://kws.go.ke/) - Official resource for exploring the Rift Valley soda lake, rhino sanctuary, and extensive birdlife.

### Amboseli & Mount Kilimanjaro Region

- [Amboseli National Park Info](https://kws.go.ke/) - Official visitor hub for viewing large elephant herds against the backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro.

### Tsavo & Coastal Region

- [Tsavo National Parks (East & West)](https://kws.go.ke/) - Official guide for Kenya's largest protected wilderness area, featuring red elephants, volcanic lava flows, and Mzima Springs.
- [Watamu Marine National Park & Reserve](https://kws.go.ke/) - Coastal marine sanctuary portal for coral reef snorkeling, turtle conservation, and white sand beaches.

### Samburu & Northern Reserves

- [Samburu National Reserve Portal](https://kws.go.ke/) - Visitor guide for Northern Kenya's arid landscapes, Ewaso Ng'iro river systems, and unique wildlife species (Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe).

## Safety & Conservation Rules

Practical advice on wildlife distance protocols, malaria precautions, emergency contacts, and park guidelines.

- [Kenya Police Emergency Services (Dial 112 / 999)](https://www.police.go.ke/) - National emergency response dispatch numbers for police, medical, and fire assistance.
- [KWS Code of Conduct for Visitors](https://kws.go.ke/) - Official rules regarding wildlife viewing distance, prohibition of animal feeding, and preservation of natural habitats.
